## HOW TO USE THIS COURSE

- There are seven modules, numbered 0 to 6. Go in order. Don't skip Module 0 or Module 4. Those two keep your money safe.  
- Every module ends with a **"Lock It In"** box. If you can't explain that box to a friend, read the module again.  
- Grab a notebook or open a free Google Sheet. You'll do math. It's easy math, but you have to actually do it.  
- **Rule of the whole course: paper trade first.** "Paper trading" means practice trading with fake money in a simulator. You don't use real money until you finish the 30-day plan in Module 6, and even then only if your numbers say you're ready.

**Words you'll see (plain meanings):**

| Word | What it actually means |
| :---- | :---- |
| Stock / share | A small piece of ownership in a company. One share = one piece. |
| Broker | The app or company that holds your account and places your buys and sells. |
| Entry | The price you buy at. |
| Stop (stop-loss) | The price where you say "I was wrong" and get out to cap the damage. |
| Target | The price where you plan to take profit. |
| Position | The trade you're in right now: how many shares, of what. |
| Position size | How many shares you buy. |
| Risk | The dollars you lose if your stop gets hit. It is NOT the total you spent. |
| Paper trading | Practice trading with fake money in a simulator. |
| Volume | How many shares traded hands. It shows how busy the action is. |
| Penny stock | A cheap, tiny-company stock, often under $5 a share. Scammers love these. |
| Drawdown | How far your account has dropped from its highest point. |
